Hi, I'm Beatriz! 👋

I'm a computational biologist with a background in Pharmaceutical Sciences, working with omics data since 2020. What I enjoy most about my work is solving problems and helping people: I love learning about different research questions, understanding the biology behind them, and figuring out which analyses can help answer them.

I'm currently a Research Scientist in the Genomic Data Science Core at Dartmouth, where I lead client projects end to end across bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, ATAC-seq and metagenomics. What motivates me most is helping researchers move their projects forward by turning complex data into meaningful, actionable results.

Workflows & computing

When I'm not working with data, I really enjoy being outside — going for long walks, exploring parks, and just spending time in nature. It's my favorite way to reset.

I also love discovering new coffee shops around the city, reading something good (usually fiction), and baking when I have the time.

Genomic Data Science Core · Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth

Research Scientist (Computational Biology) · Hanover, NH

I lead client projects end to end as the primary analyst (20+ to date), from raw data processing and downstream analysis to presenting results and reports to research groups. Projects span bulk and single-cell RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, metagenomics and single-cell multiomics across human, mouse and zebrafish data, including transcription-factor regulon inference (SCENIC), trajectory inference, copy number variant analysis of single-cell ResolveOME data, and modeling of microbial diversity and taxon abundance with linear and mixed-effects models (MaAsLin3).
